Crystal Palace processed illustrations for Muse

49 rectangular PNG illustrations extracted from 43 locally retained source photographs. Start with catalog.json for machine-readable captions, descriptions and image paths, or index.html to browse the collection offline.

images/ — the 49 processed PNGs
catalog.json — reviewed captions, descriptions, source IDs/hashes, crop coordinates, dimensions, repair notes and uncertainties
source_scans.json — relative source identifiers, hashes and geometry; source photographs are not included
unrepaired/ — three cropped before-repair illustrations for comparison, not full source photographs
repair_masks/ — masks showing the replaced background areas
repair_prompts.json — built-in imagegen prompts and compositing notes
contact_sheets/ — overview of the 49 images
thumbnails/ — browsing previews
validation.json — checks on this publication package

Preservation: the illustrations were rotated, perspective-corrected and cropped into rectangles while retaining their proportions. Figures 43, 54 and 74 use localized generated background patches. Pixels outside the masks were verified unchanged. No generated whole-image replacement is used. Mary Callinack (figure 43) has a larger left-edge loss and approximate background hatching. Some images retain creases, glare or source-photo clipping. Details are recorded per image.

Provenance: personal local absolute paths and capture metadata are excluded. Full phone photographs, raw download inventories, OCR dumps and ZIP archives remain local. Source filenames and hashes identify the private originals; they are not links to files in this checkout. Comparisons contain only the cropped historical illustrations.

Catalog caveats: dates and attributions reproduce the supplied pages, not an independent historical verification. The fire caption prints 20 November 1936; verify before publication. The Watherston and Brogden vase has a clipped caption and unknown figure number. The book title and author are not visible in the photographs and are not guessed.
