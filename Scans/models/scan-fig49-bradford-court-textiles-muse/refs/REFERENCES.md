# References — scan-fig49-bradford-court-textiles

## Primary reference (sole modelling source)

- `fig-49_bradford-court-textile-display.png` — copy of
  `Scans/images/fig-49_bradford-court-textile-display.png` (catalog figure 49,
  scan IMG_6443, page 157; sha256 of the publication copy
  `6a387acd999a14be2714572735479c02cd22b30484f53b682e1807d4658ffe46`).
- Catalog caption: "Bradford Court: exhibits for women."
- Catalog description: "Colour interior of Bradford Court showing striped
  fabrics, textile cabinets, women and a child."
- Repair status in the catalog: **none** — geometric extraction only, so no
  repaired area had to be treated as approximate evidence for this figure.

## What was taken from the image

- Overall composition of a composed ~6 m section: crested timber textile
  kiosk (left), tall boldly striped hangings under a canopy frame (centre),
  sloped-glass display counter in dark wood (right foreground), a second
  glazed cabinet behind it (right rear), pale boarded floor.
- Stripe palette of the centre hangings: red, blue, yellow, green, pink and
  white vertical stripes of varied width.
- Figures: two women in bonnets with white shawls over full skirts and green
  neck ribbons, standing at the counter; a small child beside them in a red
  coat, white stockings and a straw hat, holding a thin stick.
- The deep-red hanging banner at top centre is included **blank**: the
  original carries lettering ("WOOLLEN & WORSTED" appears on a comparable
  banner at upper left), and all lettering / signage text is omitted from
  the model.
- The Crystal Palace roof, trusses and striped columns visible in the
  background of the image were deliberately **not** modelled — the brief
  scopes this package to the court furniture and figures.

## Scale basis

- No dimension is printed in the source. Scale is **ESTIMATED** from the
  figures: the women are taken as ~1.6 m tall, and all furniture is sized
  in proportion to them as they appear in the image (kiosk ≈ 2.9 m to the
  top of its crest, striped hangings ≈ 2.2 m of drop, counter ≈ 0.9 m to
  the base of its sloped glass). See NOTES.md.
