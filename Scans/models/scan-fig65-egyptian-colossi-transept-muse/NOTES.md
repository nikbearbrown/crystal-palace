# scan-fig65-egyptian-colossi-transept — North Transept at Sydenham: Egyptian Colossi

**Source:** `fig-65_north-transept-egyptian-colossi-sydenham.png` (catalog fig. 65, scan IMG_6432).
Coloured interior with Egyptian colossi, sphinxes, palms and visitors beneath the glass
transept vault.

## Subject
A vignette composed strictly within the visible field: the pair of giant seated colossi
(Abu Simbel type) on a stepped, block-banded platform at the far end, each a **large
stylised temple figure** — seat block, legs and feet, torso, arms, nemes headdress, tall
white crown with disc, and the small standing attendant figure between the legs, all
abstracted monumental forms, NOT detailed sculpture. An avenue of recumbent sphinxes
(stylised: lion body, paws, chest, nemes head) — three left, two right — palms with
segmented trunks and frond fans, a boarded floor, small stylised visitors, and overhead
a partial section of the transept barrel vault (glass, ribs, purlins) with the
semicircular fan window behind the colossi and a hint of the side gallery at right.

## Scale
Estimated from the visitors in the view: colossi ≈7.3 m to the top of the head,
≈8.4 m including the tall crown (the ~7–8 m class the brief specifies), nearest
sphinx ≈4.6 m long, palms 6–7 m. **Scale class: estimated.** Vault section spans
≈25.6 m at the springing with a ≈18.5 m crown as a vignette element, not a surveyed
transept dimension.

## Assumptions / uncertainties
- The colossi and sphinxes are simplified masses: no carved detail, inscriptions or
  facial features are attempted or implied. Faces are blank forms.
- The right edge of the scan is clipped by the source photograph — clipped architecture
  is NOT invented; the composition stops inside the visible field, per the catalog note.
- The attendant figures between the colossi's legs follow the view (they read as small
  standing statues); rendered in stone colour as statues, interpretive.
- Palm fronds are flat fans, a deliberate low-poly treatment.
