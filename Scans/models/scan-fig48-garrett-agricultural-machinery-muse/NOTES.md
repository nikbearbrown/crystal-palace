- **Subject modelled:** the two-storey stand as a group: four fluted white columns with bases, capitals and urn finials; blue entablature fascia boards left BLANK (see assumptions); gallery floor with red top rail, post-and-X-brace railing; on the gallery a black threshing-machine body with small roof wheels and a red machine with a front spoked wheel and hopper; at ground level a portable steam engine (horizontal black boiler with brass band, firebox, chimney stub, red spoked road wheels, flywheel), a red cart body, and the foreground black machine with sloped red top and a steel roller (read as a drill/threshing tail).
