## What was read from the view

- Low raised platform with a red carpet, pale boarded floor in front.
- Brown display wall behind, deep-red curtain at the right.
- Centre: highly ornate upright piano in black lacquer with gilt and
  coloured floral inlay panels, pierced/carved top rail, visible white
  keyboard, a sheet of music on the desk, round piano stool in front.
- Right: very tall hooded porter's chair (gilt frame, deep-buttoned
  orange-red upholstery, high curved hood), a small ornate side chair in
  front of it with a small card on the seat, a tall gilt pedestal urn,
  and large round decorative trays/plaques leaning and hanging.
- Left/back: flat wall boards carrying door furniture (rows of knobs,
  finger plates, escutcheons), a large diagonal carved dark panel
  above them, and a metal firescreen/fireback panel at far left.
- Visitors, left of the piano: a woman in a blue bodice, white full
  skirt and yellow bonnet, and a man in a black tailcoat and top hat.
- Trade signs are visible in the view but are **not** reproduced: all
  sign boards in the model are blank (see NOTES.md).
