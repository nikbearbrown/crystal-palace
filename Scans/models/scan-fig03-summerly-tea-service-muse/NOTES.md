# NOTES — scan-fig03-summerly-tea-service (Muse version)

**Subject.** "Felix Summerly's" tea service — designed by Henry Cole,
manufactured by Minton & Co., bone china, 1846 (V&A), as photographed in
fig-03 of the Scans catalog. This package is the Muse version of the
subject, built for the Crystal Palace film project.

**What was modelled** — the full service AS ARRANGED in the photograph, on
two display levels (the photograph's dark stepped display stands are
included as plain arrangement aids, not as part of the service):
- Lower level, centre: globular teapot with cylindrical neck, lid with a
  crouching-bird finial, spout formed as a bird/dragon head on an S-neck,
  and a C-handle with an animal-head top terminal.
- Lower level, left: low wide tea cup (handle toward centre) with its
  saucer propped upright behind it.
- Lower level, right: taller tapered coffee cup (handle outward) with its
  saucer propped upright behind it.
- Upper level, left: large deep slop bowl. Upper level, right: tall ewer
  with ovoid body, narrow neck, wavy lip and a tall loop handle with an
  animal-head terminal.
- All pieces plain white/cream glossy porcelain, as photographed.

**Scale.** ESTIMATED from the photograph's internal proportions (the
photograph carries no scale reference), at real tableware scale: teapot
≈ 0.15 m high overall, body max Ø ≈ 0.117 m; slop bowl Ø ≈ 0.161 m; ewer
≈ 0.17 m high to the lip. Pieces are real-world sized relative to each
other as they appear in the photograph.

**Assumptions and unseen sides.**
- The photograph shows one three-quarter view. Backs of the propped
  saucers, the undersides of pieces and the exact rear profiles are
  interpreted as plain porcelain continuations of the visible forms; the
  lathe-built pieces are rotationally symmetric except for their handles
  and spouts.
- The bird finial and the animal-head terminals on spout/handles are
  stylised simplifications of the photographed porcelain ornament, not
  exact reproductions.
- Interior detail of cups/bowl is a simple inner wall, as suggested by
  the photograph; the teapot lid is a separate piece sitting on the neck.

**Counts / QC.** 9,872 triangles (Model collection, evaluated). Pieces are
named objects (Teapot, TeaCup, TeaSaucer, CoffeeCup, CoffeeSaucer,
SlopBowl, Ewer, plus handles/spouts/finial); vessel bodies share one lathe
builder. Preview rendered from a front three-quarter angle comparable to
the reference photograph, neutral grey backdrop, Cycles CPU. The preview
was audited against the photograph for arrangement (the model's first
render was mirrored and over-lit; both were corrected and re-rendered).

**Files.** `scan-fig03-summerly-tea-service.blend`,
`scan-fig03-summerly-tea-service.glb`, `preview.png`,
`build_scan_fig03_summerly_tea_service.py` (re-runnable build script).

**Manual review.** Recommended: (1) overall scale is estimated — if used
at hero distance, check against the V&A's published dimensions for the
1846 Summerly service; (2) the finial/handle animal heads are stylised.
