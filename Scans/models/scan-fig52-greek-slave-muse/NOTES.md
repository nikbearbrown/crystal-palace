# NOTES — scan-fig52-greek-slave (Muse version)

**Subject.** Hiram Powers, *The Greek Slave* (1843), marble — the statue as
photographed in fig-52 of the Scans catalog (Corcoran Gallery version).
This package is the Muse version of the subject, built for the Crystal
Palace film project.

**What was modelled.**
- A standing female figure in the statue's pose: contrapposto with weight
  on her left leg, right leg relaxed and slightly forward, head bowed and
  turned to her left, right arm hanging at her side, left arm crossing the
  front of the pelvis; wrists cuffed and linked by a slack chain.
- The drapery post at her right side: a short pillar fragment with the
  cloth thrown over it, hanging to the plinth, with a fringe of tassels at
  its top edge (as photographed).
- The low round plinth with rim bead on a wider circular socle.

**Figure treatment (deliberate).** The figure is a smooth, mannequin-like
marble form built from merged, voxel-remeshed volumes. It records the
statue's pose and proportions only: there is NO anatomical detail, no
facial features and no claim to reproduce Powers' carved surface. The head
is a separate object so its bowed/turned pose can be adjusted.

**Scale.** ESTIMATED life-size class: statue 1.65 m (feet to crown),
plinth + socle 0.10 m, total ≈ 1.75 m. The Greek Slave versions are
life-size marble figures; the photograph carries no scale reference, so
the absolute size is an estimate, proportions follow the photograph.

**Assumptions and unseen sides.**
- The scan shows one three-quarter front view. The back (hair bun and
  shoulder blades region, chain routing behind the wrists, cloth fall
  behind the post) is interpreted in the simplest way consistent with the
  visible view.
- In the original, the drapery hangs beside a post associated with the
  locket/cross motif; here the post is a plain pillar fragment and the
  cloth a smooth fall — the small devotional object itself is not modelled.
- Chain link size/count and cuff form are approximations from the visible
  slack chain.

**Counts / QC.** 19,708 triangles (Model collection, evaluated). Preview
rendered from a front three-quarter angle comparable to the reference
photograph, neutral grey backdrop, Cycles CPU.

**Files.** `scan-fig52-greek-slave.blend`, `scan-fig52-greek-slave.glb`,
`preview.png`, `build_scan_fig52_greek_slave.py` (re-runnable).

**Manual review.** Recommended if used at hero distance: the figure is an
abstracted pose record, not a sculptural reproduction — a sculptor pass
would be needed for close-ups.
