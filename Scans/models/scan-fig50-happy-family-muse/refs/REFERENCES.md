## What was taken from the image

The image governs (it is **not** the picnic scene the batch brief guessed):

- A tall **barred cage/enclosure** filling the middle of the frame, in two
  tiers divided by a heavy mid rail, with a pale interior behind the bars.
- Inside the cage, **dancing pairs** on two levels — the upper level raised
  on a step — in varied national/period dress silhouettes (top hat, turban,
  cap, shako, conical hat, bonnets).
- Above the cage, the Crystal Palace **transept fan window**: a great
  semicircular fanlight with radial spokes, a concentric inner arc and a
  central hub, flanked by two small **pennant flags** (blank — no devices).
- In front of the bars, **large foreground spectators**: a bonneted woman
  and a shako'd soldier at left, a wide-brimmed companion, a top-hatted
  gentleman with a cane at front-left, two children at centre front, a man
  at right pointing into the cage with a thin pointer, and a woman in a
  tiered flounced dress at far right.
- The printed caption lettering ("THE HAPPY FAMILY IN HYDE PARK") is **not**
  reproduced anywhere in the model.
