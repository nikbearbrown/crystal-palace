# NOTES — scan-unnumbered-watherston-vase (Muse version)

**Subject.** Ornamental vase (sculptural centrepiece) by Watherston and
Brogden, from the unnumbered arched-top illustration in the Scans catalog
(catalog figure number unknown — the caption's beginning lies outside the
source photograph). This package is the Muse version of the subject,
built for the Crystal Palace film project.

**What was modelled** (following the single printed view):
- Lathe-turned silver-gilt body: lobed foot, trumpet stem knop,
  hemispherical bowl with rope mouldings, berry garland swags and an
  abstracted mask boss on the bowl front, and a wide rim shelf with
  hanging pendants at the diagonals.
- Six standing statues of the worthies around the rim shelf, on gilt
  plinths. The text adjacent to the print names Nelson, Wellington,
  Milton, Shakespeare, Newton and Watt; the figures are PLAIN silvered
  statue forms in period dress silhouettes — no portrait likenesses are
  attempted or implied. The front figure (Shakespeare in the print,
  holding a scroll) stands before a dark niche panel with a gilt arch
  frame and curtain swags.
- Upper canopy tier: entablature ring carried on scroll brackets, draped
  swags between four corner posts topped with flame finials, a central
  drum and dome.
- A standing robed figure as the finial on the dome (abstract statue, as
  printed).
- Two seated putti on foliage mounds flanking the stem at the base —
  built as true seated figures (lap mass on the mound, torso from the
  hips, shins dropping in front).

**Scale.** ESTIMATED: overall height ~0.95 m, rim diameter 0.63 m. The
print carries no scale reference; the size is estimated for a large
exhibition centrepiece of this class. Treat as approximate — see manual
review.

**Assumptions and unseen sides.**
- The print is the ONLY view, and it is a monochrome engraving: the
  silver-gilt (parcel-gilt) colour scheme — silver figures, gilt body —
  is an interpretation of the medium, not colour evidence.
- The rear is completed by rotating the front arrangement: six statues
  evenly spaced, garlands and canopy repeated symmetrically. The real
  object's rear arrangement is unknown.
- All sculptural detail (faces, drapery folds, garland carving, the
  mask) is abstracted to smooth forms; the original is densely chased
  and modelled.
- The niche curtains and canopy drapes are simplified tube swags
  suggesting the printed drapery.

**Counts / QC.** 30,960 triangles (Model collection, evaluated).
Preview rendered frontally from a slight elevation, comparable to the
print, neutral grey backdrop, Cycles CPU, 48 samples + denoise. QC fixes
during the build: the first framing was far too close (camera pulled
back to show the whole piece); the niche panel initially sat in front of
the central statue and hid it (moved to the recess position behind the
figure); the canopy swags first overshot the entablature silhouette
(shortened to hang between the posts).

**Files.** `scan-unnumbered-watherston-vase.blend`,
`scan-unnumbered-watherston-vase.glb`, `preview.png`,
`build_scan_watherston_vase.py` (re-runnable).

**Manual review.** Recommended: (1) overall scale is estimated — the
class spans roughly 0.6–1.0 m, so check against any published dimension
for the Watherston & Brogden vase before hero use; (2) the six worthies
are interchangeable plain statues by design — do not label individual
figures as portraits in downstream use; (3) sculptural abstraction is
heavy — a sculptor pass would be needed for close-ups.
