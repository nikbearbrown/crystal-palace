## What was taken from the image

The image governs (it contains **no armour stands** — the batch brief's
guess — and no armour at all):

- A single **cannon** on a two-wheeled carriage with a ground trail,
  barrel pointing right, displayed centre-right under a **canopy** whose
  front edge carries a scalloped valance; a small stack of round shot
  beside the carriage.
- **Two tall soldiers** in shakos standing guard at the left of the piece.
- A crowd of **schoolgirls in bonnets and long aprons** across the
  foreground, mostly seen from behind as they face the cannon (the two at
  the left edge turn outward in the print), with an adult **chaperone** in
  bonnet and shawl at the right edge.
- The two small faces the print floats in the background behind the cannon
  are an engraver's shorthand, not free-standing heads; they are **not**
  modelled as objects (an early draft's floating heads were removed).
- The printed caption lettering ("PEACE!—A SKETCH FROM THE CRYSTAL
  PALACE") is **not** reproduced anywhere in the model.

## Scale basis

- No dimension is printed in the source. Scale is **ESTIMATED** (see

  as drawn), the cannon ~2.9 m long overall on a 1.08 m wheel diameter,
  the girls 1.06–1.52 m; canopy ~6.3 m wide, vignette ~8 m.
