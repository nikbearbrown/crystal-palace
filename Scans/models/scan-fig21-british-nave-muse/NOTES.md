# scan-fig21-british-nave — Western (British) Nave of the Crystal Palace, 1851

**Source:** `fig-21_western-british-nave-crystal-palace.png` (catalog fig. 21, scan IMG_6463).
Colour view along the nave with visitors, exhibits, galleries and the glazed roof.

## Subject
A self-contained nave vignette, 1½ bays long: paired rows of slender iron columns,
galleries on both sides with balustrades and heraldic banners hanging below the gallery
edge (plain two-tone banners — the painted arms are not reproduced), and a partial
barrel-vault section overhead (glass surface with ribs and purlins, ending in lattice
tie-trusses) rather than the whole roof. Exhibits as in the view, simplified:
the great lighthouse lens apparatus on its stand (foreground left), a gilded tiered
fountain (centre), bronze busts on red plinths, a long red display table, a glass
display case, and the dark domed pavilion exhibit in the distance. Six stylised
visitors on the floor and three on the gallery — interpretive figures, not likenesses.

## Scale
Documented palace module used as the basis: 24 ft bay = 7.315 m; vignette length
1½ bays = 10.97 m; nave width 72 ft = 21.95 m; gallery level ≈24 ft = 7.3 m.
Vault crown ≈17 m: a shallowed segment chosen for the vignette — the real nave vault
profile is an interpretation here, flagged as such.

## Assumptions / uncertainties
- The colour view flattens the roof into a lattice grid; the barrel-vault section
  follows the documented building form, not the print's perspective.
- The far exhibits (fountain tiers, pavilion) are simplified silhouettes of what's
  visible; small ornament is omitted, not invented.
- The photographic glare at the scan's lower right is photographic and ignored,
  per the catalog note.
- Gallery structure behind the slab (brackets) is omitted.
