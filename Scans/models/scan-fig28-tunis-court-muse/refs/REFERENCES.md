## What was read from the engraving

- A row of shallow, round-arched alcoves on slim piers along the left,
  forming a bazaar-like display wall.
- First alcove: back wall hung with round flat straw hats (discs with raised
  crowns) in a row, decorative fans/plates beside them, and rows of small
  plates below; a counter in front with a dark vertically striped/patterned
  front, crowded with vessels — tall ewers/pitchers with spouts, bottles,
  a conical-lidded jar, bowls.
- Second alcove: a stack of folded textiles/rugs with patterned borders,
  hanging cloths/garments behind, and a seated figure among them in the
  neighbouring alcove.
- Third alcove (partial): more hanging fabrics and folded goods.
- Large carpets hanging over the top of the alcove wall (top left) and a
  very large draped carpet swag in a catenary across the upper right.
- Right: a tall glazed display cabinet with a thin frame and glass panes,
  containing hanging/stacked goods; square-motif patterned panels nearby.
- Two standing men in dark coats at centre-right — used as the scale
  reference (~1.70 m each).
- Foreground: a patterned carpet/mat runner on the floor; at right a large
  dark animal hide draped over a low support, its tail end trailing on the
  floor toward the centre; at left, stringed instruments (pear-bodied
  lute/oud forms with long necks) leaning against the counter and a shallow
  metal bowl on the floor.
- The Crystal Palace iron-and-glass roof is visible in the engraving
  background only and is **not** modelled, per the brief.
