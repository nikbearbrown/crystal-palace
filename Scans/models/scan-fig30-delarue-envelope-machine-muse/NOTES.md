# NOTES — De La Rue's envelope machine (fig. 30) — MUSE VERSION

**Muse version.** Staged locally as `scan-fig30-delarue-envelope-machine/`; ships as `scan-fig30-delarue-envelope-machine-muse/` under Scans/models/.

- **Source:** `fig-30_de-la-rue-envelope-machine-demonstration.png` (scan IMG_6457), p.107. Catalog description: "Visitors watch an attendant demonstrate envelope-making machinery at a colourful stationery stand." Catalog lists no uncertainties; repair: none.
- **Subject modelled:** De La Rue's stationery stand: red back wall with gold cornice and two dark display boards gridded with white envelopes (abstract, no lettering added), a row of coloured paper rolls, coloured paper/book stacks, white rosette paper fans, a glazed display counter with coloured books and an open sample book, and — right of centre — the envelope machine itself: cast-iron table frame on shaped legs, bed with a paper sheet, flywheel, upright press frame with head, platen and hand lever. A capped attendant stands at the machine; two stylised visitors (top-hat man, bonneted woman in green) watch from beyond the brass rope rail on its green carpet.
- **Scale:** ESTIMATED from the figures (attendant 1.70 m). Machine bed height ~1.0 m; stand wall 2.9 m high as painted (the painted stand is a partial set, not a full room).
- **Assumptions / interpretation:** only the machine's exterior press form is visible in the painting; the internal envelope-folding mechanism and any gearing below the bed are NOT shown and are represented only by the flywheel/crank — treat mechanism as interpretation, not evidence. Signage lettering visible in the painting ("THOS DE LA RUE AND COMPANY" etc.) is deliberately not reproduced as geometry; display boards are abstract grids. The Palace staircase and hall behind the stand are excluded. Figures are simplified period-dress forms — no likenesses.
- **Triangles:** 7,324 (115 mesh objects). Blender 4.5.3, metres, origin at base centre, scale applied. GLB excludes the render ground/lights/camera.
- **QC:** preview compared against the reference (front-left three-quarter). Fix rounds: attendant repositioned so he reads beside the press instead of being hidden behind it; counter glazing slope reduced; bonnet colour corrected to brown. First build attempt timed out mid-render under heavy parallel load and was re-run cleanly at reduced samples + denoise.
- **Manual review:** the exact De La Rue envelope-machine mechanism (a known patented design) could be modelled more faithfully from patent sources if a hero asset is ever needed; this version stays with what the painting shows.
