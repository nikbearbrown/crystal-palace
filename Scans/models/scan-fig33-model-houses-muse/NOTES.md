# scan-fig33-model-houses — Prince Albert's Model Houses for Four Families

**Source:** `fig-33_prince-albert-model-working-class-home-1851.png` (catalog fig. 33, scan IMG_6455).
ILN engraving, 14 June 1851; Henry Roberts's design for the Society for Improving the
Condition of the Labouring Classes, erected at Prince Albert's initiative beside the
Great Exhibition.

## Subject
The engraved exterior: a symmetrical two-storey block with pale rendered walls and brick
dressings — brick quoins, string courses and window surrounds; three shaped (Dutch-style)
front gables, each with a roundel and a small finial; the centre bay recessed as an open
stair gallery: ground-floor porch with slender columns, first-floor gallery with an iron
railing. Large sash windows (diamond lattice glazing suggested by mullion crosses only).
Tall brick chimney stacks (left stack emphasised as in the engraving). Low front wall with
stone coping, gate piers with ball finials and a simple iron gate. Chimney/roof behind the
gables is a pitched slate roof — the engraving barely shows it, so its pitch is a
reasonable historical interpretation. Three stylised street figures give scale;
they are interpretive, not likenesses.

## Scale
Estimated from the engraving plus the known building type: width 13.2 m, depth 7.6 m,
eaves ≈6.2 m, gable tops 7.35–7.75 m. **Scale class: estimated** — the original was a
compact four-family block; exact dimensions were not taken from a measured drawing.

## Assumptions / uncertainties
- Rear elevation unseen: modelled plain with the same window rhythm (interpretation).
- Roof pitch and chimneys' rear placement are interpreted; the engraving shows the left
  stack taller than the others and this is followed.
- The centre roundel in the engraving appears to read "1851"; no lettering is modelled.
- Interior stair is only hinted (dark mass inside the porch), not modelled as a staircase.
- Brick courses are a procedural Blender brick texture on trim elements only; the main
  walls are smooth render as engraved.

## Build

- Triangles: 6,290 (under the ~50k budget).
- Origin at base centre, metres, scale applied. Preview: front three-quarter from the
