## Text / dates

- **First playing of the great fountains: 18 June 1856**; Brunel's
  water towers operational 2 June 1856 (Historic England, via the
  round-2 review, `references/muse-round2/review/GAPS-FOUND.md`,
  scene notes S51/S60 and Contradictions). This is the prop's date
  gate: the basin in operating state belongs to c.1856–57 (CP042),
  never to the June 1854 opening.
- The June 1854 Science Museum lithograph of the inauguration
  (round 2, S55-02) shows the fountains playing and both towers
  complete — **anticipatory, not evidence**; not used for this prop.
- Palace length 1,608 ft: Phillips, *Guide to the Crystal Palace
  and Park* (1854), corroborated by the 1856 guide (round-2 review
  §B5). The two guides disagree on heights, not lengths.
- No dimensioned drawing of any Sydenham fountain basin was found
  in either collection round (round-2 review §B3: "no separate
  1854–57 terrace/fountain engineering plan was found"). All
  absolute dimensions of this prop are therefore plan-ratio
  estimates — see NOTES.md.
