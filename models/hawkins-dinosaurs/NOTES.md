# Hawkins Crystal Palace dinosaurs — sculpture pack (CANDIDATE)

Procedural rebuild of the main 1854 Benjamin Waterhouse Hawkins secondary-island
figures as they stand in Crystal Palace Park today (and stood in 1854): two
Iguanodons (standing quadruped with nose horn; recumbent), Megalosaurus
(quadruped with shoulder hump), Hylaeosaurus (dorsal spines), plesiosaurs
(long necks), ichthyosaurs (waterline, sclerotic-ring eyes).

## Status
**CANDIDATE** — not promoted to live hero renders. Keep/revert is owner call.

## Finish
Painted cement: stone-grey / green-grey with mottling and scale bump (matches
restored park finish in Muse + Commons photos). Not clay-beige blobs.

## Units
Metres. Origin under body centre, ground at z≈0 for land animals; marine
forms origin near waterline belly.
