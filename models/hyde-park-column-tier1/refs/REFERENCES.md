# References — hyde-park-column-tier1

All paths are relative to the repo root (`nikbearbrown/crystal-palace`),
unless noted as a crop derived from an in-repo scan. No outside
copyrighted text or images were used. Hyde Park 1851 only — no
Sydenham evidence is used in this model.

## Primary source (in repo)

- `references/downes-1852/source-scans/plates/plate-008.jpg` —
  Downes Plate VIII, "Details of columns" (John Weale, Jan 1852).
  Fig. 3/4/5 full columns, Fig. 12 girder connection (enlarged),
  Fig. 13 flange plan. This plate is the geometric authority for
  the prop. Crops derived from it are in this folder:
  - `plate-viii-fig3-full-column.jpg` — Fig. 3, full column
    (floor to girder, 19 ft 2 in to girder bottom).
  - `plate-viii-fig12-girder-connection.jpg` — Fig. 12, girder
    connection (3 ft girder depth, triangular web profile).
  - `plate-viii-scale-bars.jpg` — scale bars from the same plate.

## Dimension controls (in repo)

- `references/downes-1852/crystal-palace-1851/CONTROL-DIMENSIONS.md`
  — D06 (24 ft bay), D10 (3 ft girder), D11 (22 ft 2 in girder top).
- `references/downes-1852/crystal-palace-1851/MEASUREMENTS.md` —
  printed-page heights and wall-thickness range (⅜–1¼ in).
- `references/downes-1852/crystal-palace-1851/PLATE-INDEX.md` —
  plate identification (Plate VIII = Details of columns).

## Cross-check only (geometry NOT copied)

- `course-models/crystal-palace-1851-claude/scripts/parts.py` —
  used only to confirm the 18 ft 11¾ in lower-column height.
  This prop was built independently from the plates.

## Appearance check (in repo, not a dimension source)

- 1851 interior photographs in `references/muse-drive/images/`
  (e.g. 0169/0170/0177/0181/0184) — used only to confirm that
  columns appear light / painted, not bare metal. The exact 1851
  paint colour is NOT verified; see NOTES.md.
