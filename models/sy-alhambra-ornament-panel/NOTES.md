# NOTES — Alhambra Court arch panel (simplified)

Prop for the Crystal Palace film. A flat wall panel for
Sydenham court dressing: two piers, slender columns, a
pointed Moorish arch with a stylised muqarnas fringe,
and a patterned frieze band above.

## Source — one view, and its limits

The ONLY source used is the in-repo view
`references/muse-drive/images/commons/Crystal_Palace_Alhambra_Court.jpg`
(453×575, public domain, "Entrance to the Alhambra Court
by Owen Jones") — a tinted lithograph of the Court of
Lions entrance. Verified from it: the portal form
(pointed horseshoe-type arch, slender columns, patterned
frieze above) and a warm-stone impression. NOT verified:
the exact arch profile, muqarnas geometry, or the
court's real polychromy. The arch here is a two-centre
pointed approximation (1.8 m radius, centres ±0.6 m),
the muqarnas is stepped blocks, the frieze diamonds are
a simplified alternating pattern, and colours are a
lithograph impression. Caption any use as a simplified
panel from a single view — never as Owen Jones's surveyed
decoration.
