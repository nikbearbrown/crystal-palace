# References — sy-alhambra-ornament-panel

All sources are already in the repo (`nikbearbrown/crystal-palace`);
nothing was copied from outside it. Sydenham 1854 evidence only.

## Primary sources used

1. **Coloured print, "Entrance to the Alhambra Court"** —
   `court-entrance-lead-0108.jpg`
   Repo: `references/muse-drive/images/commons/Crystal_Palace_Alhambra_Court.jpg`
   (round-1 catalogue no. **0108**, the SCENE-BUILD-PLAN's "image lead
   0108"; manifest licence: Public domain). The only view of the
   Alhambra Court in the collection. Hand-coloured; small (453 × 575).
   Used for: the court's entrance composition (arch + ornamented wall)
   and the attested colour register (warm gold/cream stucco, dark
   blue-black inscription bands) — see the enlarged crops
   `crop-colour-arch-top.png` and `crop-colour-pier-right.png`.

2. **1856 guide, p.66 — woodcut "Entrance to Alhambra Court"** —
   `guide-1856-p66-entrance-woodcut.jpg`
   Repo: `references/muse-drive/images/commons/Guide_to_the_Crystal_Palace_and_Park_1856_(147900443).jpg`
   The same view as (1), at full resolution. This is the pattern
   source: the stacked quatrefoil medallions and inscription bands of
   the entrance piers, the zigzag dentil band, the diamond-lattice
   dado, the sebka diaper of the upper walls, and (in the interior
   view through the arch) the roundel frieze band above the Court of
   the Lions arches and the same diamond dado inside the court.
   Enlargements: `crop-pier-medallions-dado.png`,
   `crop-dado-lattice.png`, `crop-interior-wall.png`,
   `crop-frieze-spandrel.png`.

3. **1856 guide, p.67 — "Ground Plan of the Alhambra"** —
   `guide-1856-alhambra-plan-0246.jpg`
   Repo: `.../Guide_to_the_Crystal_Palace_and_Park_1856_(147900444).jpg`
   (round-1 catalogue no. **0246**). Court layout only: Court of the
   Lions, Hall of Justice, Hall of the Abencerrages, Divans, Cast
   Room. No panel dimension is given on it.

4. **1856 guide, pp.68–70 — Alhambra Court text** —
   `guide-1856-p68-alhambra-text.jpg` (147900445),
   `guide-1856-p69-alhambra-text.jpg` (147900447),
   `guide-1856-p70-alhambra-text.jpg` (147900448).
   Used for: "the inside was literally covered, from end to end, with
   rich arabesque work in coloured stucco" (p.68); walls "covered
   with diaper work, consisting of inscriptions in Arabic character,
   of conventional representation of flowers and of flowing
   decoration" (p.68); the court is 75 ft long (p.68); the Court of
   Lions columns and arches are "of the actual size of the original"
   (p.69); "the patterns on the walls and ceilings being taken from
   other portions of the Alhambra" and the court built from Owen
   Jones's original casts of Alhambra ornaments brought from Spain
   (p.70).

## What is NOT in the collection (searched, absent)

- Owen Jones's *Plans, Elevations, Sections and Details of the
  Alhambra* and his *Grammar of Ornament* plates: **not downloaded**
  in round 1 (a Grammar plate is a skipped-budget manifest entry
  only). No Jones plate was used; the pattern here is read from the
  court's own views (sources 1–2) as instructed by the build plan.
- Jones's 1854 handbook *The Alhambra Court in the Crystal Palace*:
  present in the manifest only as a skipped-media PDF entry; not in
  the repo and not used.
- Any photograph of the Alhambra Court (Delamotte or other): none in
  the round-1 catalogue; source 1 is a coloured print, not a photo.
- Any dimensioned drawing of a court wall or panel: none. See
  NOTES.md for how the panel's size was adopted and flagged.
