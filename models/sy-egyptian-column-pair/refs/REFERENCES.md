# REFERENCES — sy-egyptian-column-pair

All sources are from the repo's own round-1 collection
(`references/muse-drive/`), copied here from the identical local
mirror of that collection. Licences are the round-1 manifest's
(`references/muse-drive/data/manifest.csv`). No source outside the
repo collection was used for geometry. Round 2
(`references/muse-round2/`, branch `muse-round2`) was also checked:
it contains no Egyptian Court material (its only court photograph is
the Roman Court, S57-02 — a different court, not used here).

## Primary sources (geometry and form)

- `guide-1856-p51-column-from-karnak.jpg` — 1856 *Guide to the
  Crystal Palace and Park* (Samuel Phillips), printed p.51, with the
  captioned woodcut **"Column from Karnak"**: the column of the
  court's Hall of Columns drawn in elevation — round disc plinth,
  petal collar at the shaft foot, relief-carved tapering shaft with
  horizontal register bands, banded necking, ribbed cluster-bud
  capital, square abacus, hieroglyph frieze and cavetto cornice
  above. This is the prop's form source. Public domain (manifest).
  Repo path: `references/muse-drive/images/commons/Guide_to_the_Crystal_Palace_and_Park_1856_(147900421).jpg`.
  Enlarged crop: `crop-column-from-karnak.png`.
- `guide-1856-p47-egyptian-court-plan.jpg` — same guide, printed
  p.47: **"Ground Plan of Egyptian Court"** (round-1 gallery 0226).
  Shows the "Hall of Karnac Columns" as three rows of columns
  (facade row of 6, two rows of 7) between two "Facade of the Hall
  of Columns" lines, the Tomb from Beni Hassan, the Court of
  Amunsthph, the Egyptian Museum and the Model of Temple of Aboo
  Simbel. Column pitch and square size measured on this plan.
  Public domain (manifest).
  Repo path: `.../Guide_to_the_Crystal_Palace_and_Park_1856_(147900416).jpg`.
  Enlarged crop: `crop-egyptian-court-plan-0226.png`.

## Text evidence (same guide, read at full resolution)

- `guide-1856-p48-facade-capitals-text.jpg` — printed p.48: the
  temple fronting the nave is "not taken from any one particular
  structure, but composed from various sources, to illustrate
  Egyptian columns and capitals during the Ptolemaic period"; "The
  capitals or heads of the columns are palm- and lotus-leaved; some
  showing the papyrus in its various stages of development, from
  the simple bud to the full-blown flower"; on the walls "coloured
  sunk-reliefs"; the frieze inscription names Victoria. (Facade
  evidence — a different column group from the Hall of Karnak pair
  modelled here; see NOTES.md.)
  Repo path: `.../Guide_to_the_Crystal_Palace_and_Park_1856_(147900417).jpg`.
- `guide-1856-p49-first-order-column-and-amunsthph.jpg` — printed
  p.49: the Court of Amunsthph colonnade — "The columns represent
  eight stems and buds of the [lotus] bound together, and are cast
  from a black granite column [bearing] the name of Amunsthph, now
  in the British Museum"; and the Beni Hassan tomb with the
  captioned woodcut "First order of Egyptian Column" (a fluted
  proto-Doric column — another distinct column type in the court,
  not the pair modelled). Repo path: `.../147900418....jpg`.
- `guide-1856-p50-karnak-heights-and-hathor.jpg` — printed p.50:
  "nearly all the models here introduced are very much below the
  size of the architectural remains which they represent. For
  example: the majority of the columns in the temple of Karnak are
  47 feet high, and some are 62 feet"; "the beautiful lotus columns
  to the left, surmounted by the cow-eared Goddess of Love of the
  Egyptians" (Hathor-headed capitals — mentioned in one clause,
  not illustrated; see NOTES.md on why the Karnak pair was chosen).
  Also the colonnade "from the Island of Philöe". Repo path:
  `.../147900420....jpg`.
- p.51 text (on `guide-1856-p51-column-from-karnak.jpg`): the Hall
  of Columns columns "are [re]duced models of a portion of the
  celebrated Temple of Karnak at [Thebes]... commenced by Osirei
  the First, and completed by his [son] Rameses the Great."

## Position only

- `guide-1856-ground-plan-0205.jpg` — 1856 guide ground plan of the
  whole palace (round-1 gallery 0205); the key lists "12 Egyptian
  Court, 12A Sculptures, 12B Tomb of Beni Hassan, 13 Model of the
  Aboo Simbel Tomb". Used ONLY for the court's position in the
  building, per the build plan. Public domain (manifest).
  Repo path: `.../Guide_to_the_Crystal_Palace_and_Park_1856_(147900384).jpg`.

## Checked and NOT used

- Delamotte photographs: the round-1 collection's filenames and
  manifest were searched for an Egyptian Court view (Delamotte's
  *Views of the Crystal Palace and Park, Sydenham*). None is in the
  collection — the Delamotte views held are the nave, sculpture
  gallery, opening ceremony and exteriors (the Roman Court plate of
  the same album was added in round 2). The dated visual evidence
  for this prop is therefore the 1856 guide's own woodcuts and
  text, and NOTES.md says so wherever that limits certainty.
- The guide's p.48/49 engraving "The Gigantic Figures of Rameses
  the Great" depicts the original at Abu Simbel in Egypt, not the
  Sydenham court; not used as court evidence.
- `-Assyrian_Court...MET_DP323102.jpg` (round 1) is the Assyrian
  Court — a different court; not used.
