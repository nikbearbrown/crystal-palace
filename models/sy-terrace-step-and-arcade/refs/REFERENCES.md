## Published description used (quoted, not copied into the model)

Historic England list entry 1064352, *The Upper and Lower Terrace
of the Crystal Palace Gardens* (legacy description, Grade II,
first listed 1953): the terraces were "designed by Paxton at the
time of the re-erection"; "Each terrace consists of a series of
round-headed arches containing semi-circular semi-domed niches
arranged in groups of 3 with taller pilasters between the groups
**carried up into the balustrade above which is of similar
pattern of narrow round-headed arches**, the taller pilasters
topped by vases or statues. Flights of steps lead down from each
terrace at intervals."

This is the decisive evidence that the terrace "balustrade" is a
**pierced arcaded parapet** — not a row of turned balusters.
