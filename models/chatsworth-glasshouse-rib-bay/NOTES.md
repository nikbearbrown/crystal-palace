# NOTES — Chatsworth glasshouse rib bay (illustrative)

Prop for the Crystal Palace film. One glasshouse bay:
three curved ribs on a semicircular arc, five purlins,
glazing bars and glass panes, on two low side walls.

## Labelled illustrative — read this first

This is NOT a surveyed Chatsworth room and NOT Palace
geometry. No Chatsworth dimension was sourced. Palace rib
figures (13⅝ in wide, 9 ft 6 in long, 37-ft radius, from
the Downes controls) were deliberately NOT reused here.
The lily-leaf inspiration anecdote is omitted by the
storyboard and no lily-leaf prop is implied. Caption any
use as an illustrative glasshouse bay.
