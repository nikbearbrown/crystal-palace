## Primary text source (not copied — external scan)

- *Official Descriptive and Illustrated Catalogue of the Great
  Exhibition* (1851), **Class XXIV (Glass), exhibit No. 20 — F. & C.
  Osler**: "Large fountain, in cut crystal glass, **27 feet high**.
  (At the intersection of the Main Avenue and Transept.)" — the
  controlling dimension, 27 ft = 8.23 m. e-rara scan:
  https://www.e-rara.ch/download/pdf/6854827.pdf
- Basin diameter 24 ft (7.32 m): Birmingham Post / Free Library,
  "How our glassware once lit the world" — adopted over the plate's
  ≈26–27 ft photo ratio; see spike §4 conflict note and NOTES.md.

## Not used (state separation)

- The Sydenham re-erection (post-1854, in a goldfish pool — Negretti
  & Zambra photograph, Yale Center for British Art,
  alma:99159373603408651) is a **different installation state** and
  was not blended into this Hyde Park 1851 model. The fountain was
  destroyed in the 1936 fire (spike §3).
