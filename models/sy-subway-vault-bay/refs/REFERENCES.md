# REFERENCES — sy-subway-vault-bay

All sources are Sydenham / High Level station material. No Hyde Park
evidence is used, and nothing here is dated to 1854 — the subway is
1860s fabric (opened 23 December 1865).

## Published descriptions used for construction facts (not dimensions)

- **Historic England, National Heritage List entry 1385457** —
  "Subway, Vestibule, Terrace and Stairs to the Crystal Palace":
  opened 1865, designed by Charles Barry Junior; "the subway has
  three parallel rows of five octagonal columns, with half-octagonal
  responds against oblong piers at either end and along the walls;
  all are cream brick with moulded stone capitals and ovolo stone
  mouldings to the brick bases. Circular fan vaults rise from each
  in red and cream brick laid to form diamonds. Ovolo-moulded stone
  defines each vault, between which are circular roundels with
  polychromatic diapering, and a central cast-iron gas light
  fitting. The side walls are cream brick between the piers, with a
  border of red brick." Materials: red and cream brick; paved floors
  in alternating stone. (Text as reproduced in the Thomas Ford &
  Partners Design & Access Statement, Bromley planning application,
  June 2021, appendix.)
- **Friends of Crystal Palace Subway** (welcome leaflet): the brick
  vault is "little more than 30 cm thick at the top of the arch" and
  still supports the four-lane road above — the source of the model's
  0.30 m vault thickness.
- **Thomas Ford & Partners, Design & Access Statement (2021)**: the
  subway officially opened 23 December 1865; High Level station
  opened August 1865 and closed 1954 — the dates behind the CP046 /
  CP065 / CP071 state dressing this bay is built to serve.
