# NOTES — Twisted / collapsed iron fragment, 1936

Prop for the Crystal Palace film. Sydenham post-1936-fire
state only — never an intact component, never Hyde Park 1851.

## What it is

A standing column stub broken at ~2 m with a jagged top, a
kinked fallen girder section on the ground beside it, and a
fallen column length. Origin at ground centre. Units: metres.
Blender 4.2+. Seeded random (1936), so the jagged break is
reproducible.

## What this prop must NOT be used for

- Never reuse it as an intact column or girder — it is
  labelled ruin, and the film's CP062 rule (morning after:
  twisted remains on the ridge, towers standing beyond the
  missing roof) depends on that distinction.
- Not a surveyed remain: no post-fire survey measurement was
  used, and no specific member, bay or collapse sequence is
  claimed.
- CP062 must not use a fully cleared plateau for the morning
  after — this fragment exists so the morning-after shot has
  debris in it.
