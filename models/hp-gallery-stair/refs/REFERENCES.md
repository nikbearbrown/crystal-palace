# REFERENCES — hp-gallery-stair

All evidence is from the repo `nikbearbrown/crystal-palace` (branch
`main`), already collected there; nothing was taken from outside the
repo. Downes plates are from Charles Downes, *The Building for the
Great Exhibition* (John Weale, Jan 1852) — public domain engineering
plates of the as-built 1851 Hyde Park palace.

## Full plates copied into this folder

- `plate-002.jpg` — Downes Plate II, "Plan of Galleries". Locates the
  stair bays against the gallery voids (controls D23–D26 in
  CONTROL-DIMENSIONS.md) and shows where the flights interrupt the
  galleries. Repo path:
  `references/downes-1852/source-scans/plates/plate-002.jpg`.
- `plate-015.jpg` — Downes Plate XV, staircase plans and the
  "Section through Staircase on line a.a". Primary source for the
  flight arrangement (two flights + mid landing), tread numbering
  1–21 / 22–42, the wavy gallery/lattice landing rail, the straight
  X-lattice flight balustrade, scroll handrail ends and the landing
  columns. Repo path: `.../plates/plate-015.jpg`.
- `plate-016.jpg` — Downes Plate XVI, staircase ironwork details:
  balustrade panels G/H, the handrail scroll L, tread bearers I on
  brackets K, the string A and its bolted joint to the landing joist
  (joist printed "7 × 2½"), landing column details (Figs. 4/6), and
  the "Section at g.g" through the string/handrail splice (⅞ in
  bolts at 8 in centres). Repo path: `.../plates/plate-016.jpg`.
- `plate-017.jpg` — Downes Plate XVII, gallery railing details:
  Fig. 12 the wavy-bar lattice elevation (bars R, lozenge bosses S,
  handrail L, floor plate T, frame standard N), Fig. 7/8 the standard
  post M (printed 3 ft 1⅞ in + 6¼ in finial; base plate 6½ in sq,
  bore 4¾ in, wall ⅜ in), Fig. 10 the section through a standard at
  the gallery edge (timber handrail, curved stay to the joist).
  Repo path: `.../plates/plate-017.jpg`.

## Crops used while modelling (derived from the plates above)

- `plate-015-crop-section.jpg` — the a.a section, for the string
  depth, handrail height and rail-height measurements.
- `plate-015-crop-plan.jpg` — the general plan: step numbering,
  landing columns, flight widths.
- `plate-016-crop-newel-string.jpg` — panels G/H, scroll, tread
  bearers, landing joint.
- `plate-017-crop-rail-fig12.jpg` — the lattice elevation, for the
  diamond pitch, wave period and boss rows.
- `plate-017-crop-standard-fig7-8.jpg` — post M elevation and base
  plan, with the printed dimensions.
- `plate-017-crop-rail-section-fig10.jpg` — standard section at the
  gallery edge (timber handrail grain visible).

## Not used

- The course model's stairs (`course-models/`) were not consulted:
  the plate-printed controls above fix every primary dimension of
  this prop, so no dimension had to be taken from a model the
  storyboard flags as simplified. No geometry was copied from any
  existing model.
- Sydenham evidence of any kind — none. This is the Hyde Park 1851
  stair only.
