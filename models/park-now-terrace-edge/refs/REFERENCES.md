## Published description used (quoted, not copied into the model)

Historic England list entry 1064352, *The Upper and Lower Terrace
of the Crystal Palace Gardens* (Grade II): "Each terrace consists
of a series of round-headed arches containing semi-circular
semi-domed niches arranged in groups of 3 with taller pilasters
between the groups **carried up into the balustrade above which is
of similar pattern of narrow round-headed arches**, the taller
pilasters topped by vases or statues."

## Dimension reuse

All parapet dimensions, the pilaster width and the bay length are
reused unchanged from the shipped evidence-derived terrace module
`models/sy-terrace-step-and-arcade/` (this stream's build; its
NOTES.md carries the full published / photo-counted /
photo-proportioned table). This prop adds the terrace **wall**
below that parapet, the plateau and forecourt surfaces, the empty
plinths and the modern fence — the parts the step module
explicitly left unbuilt.
