# Sources — cast-iron standards A–D

All reference material is already in this repo (per MUSE-PROMPT.md rules).

- `plate-020a.jpg` — full copy of `references/downes-1852/source-scans/plates/plate-020a.jpg`:
  Downes, *The Building Erected in Hyde Park for the Great Exhibition of the
  Works of Industry of All Nations, 1851* (John Weale, 1852), Plate 20a,
  "Details of cast-iron standards A–D" (plate not in the book's List of Plates;
  subject from its own figure captions — see
  `references/downes-1852/crystal-palace-1851/PLATE-INDEX.md`).
  Scan via Internet Archive (McGill Library copy). **Licence: public domain**
  (published 1850s), as recorded for the Downes plates in
  `references/muse-drive/data/manifest.csv`.
- Figure crops from the same plate, made for this build:
  - `fig07-standard-A-strap.jpg` — Fig. 7 area: one strap of end standard A, face view.
  - `fig13-standard-B-side.jpg` — Fig. 13: standard B, side elevation.
  - `fig14-standard-C-side.jpg` — Fig. 14: standard C, side elevation.
  - `fig18-standard-D-front.jpg` — Fig. 18: standard D, front elevation.
  - `fig21-22-standard-A-foot-bracket.jpg` — Figs. 21/22: foot bracket M details for A.

Also consulted (in repo, not copied here):
`references/downes-1852/crystal-palace-1851/PLATE-INDEX.md`,
`CONTROL-DIMENSIONS.md` and `MEASUREMENTS.md` — neither control document
carries dimensions for the standards, so all sizing comes from Plate 20a itself
(its printed scale bar and the dimensions written on its figures).
