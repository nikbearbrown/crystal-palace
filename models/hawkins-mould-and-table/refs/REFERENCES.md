## Text/fact sources consulted † (not copied)

- **ILN, 5 November 1853** (report of the royal visit to the model-room):
  the Iguanodon "in clay — a vast scaly lizard, **thirty feet long and
  eleven feet high**". Via profjoecain.net's transcription of the ILN
  report. This is the size anchor for the mould.
- **Hawkins's lecture of 27 May 1854**, quoted on victorianweb.org:
  the standing Iguanodon's bill of materials — "4 iron columns 9 feet
  long by 7 inches diameter, 600 bricks, 650 5-inch half-round
  drain-tiles, 900 plain tiles, 38 casks of cement, 90 casks of broken
  stone … 640 bushels of artificial stone … 100 feet of iron hooping".
  (Finished-model construction; the iron hooping also motivates the
  hoops on this mould case — see NOTES, "inferred".)
- **Owen / Rudwick**, via victorianweb.org: Owen and twenty others sat
  inside the mould; **an overflow of seven guests sat on a platform
  raised to the same level just outside it**. Modern retellings vary
  (11–14 inside); the ILN's own "one-and-twenty … in the body" is
  followed in NOTES, with this noted as the secondary account.
- **Earth Magazine, "Benchmarks: December 31, 1853"** and the History
  Press: dinner staging details — steps up to a platform built around
  the model, a pink-and-white tent above, chandelier and candelabra,
  banners naming Cuvier, Mantell, Conybeare, Buckland. (The tent and
  banners are scene dressing, not part of this prop — see NOTES.)

## Searched for, NOT held in the repo

- **The invitation card itself.** Round 2 records that no second,
  independent period record of the dinner was found (GAPS-FOUND S53).
  The card survives in museum/manuscript collections and is described
  in the ILN report (ref 2): the invitation text written on the wing
  of a Pterodactyle spread before an etching of the Iguanodon "with his
  socially-loaded stomach". Surviving-card wording (Earth Magazine):
  "…at dinner in the mould of the Iguanodon at the Crystal Palace on
  Saturday evening December the thirty first 1853 at five o'clock"
  (the ILN's own quotation of the card says four p.m. — the sources
  disagree; neither is asserted in the model).
- **No dimensioned drawing of the mould, the workshop, or the staging**
  exists in either round of the collection. All mould dimensions below
  the headline animal size are derived or estimated — see NOTES.md.
