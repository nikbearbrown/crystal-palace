# References — hp-great-elm

No reference image is copied here, deliberately: no image in
`references/` gives a dimensioned elm, and a transept photograph
must not be used to claim a specific tree's height or girth.

- Storyboard v001, CP018–CP023: the Hyde Park building
  encloses mature park elms in the transept. That is the only
  claim this prop rests on — that elms were there, enclosed.
- Downes controls and the 1856 guide dimensions checked: no
  elm height, girth or crown measurement was found.
- Transept interior views in `references/muse-drive/images/`
  (commons / main-article) show elms only as background
  silhouettes; they were used as an appearance sanity check
  (a tall trunked tree with a broad crown), not as a source
  for any number in NOTES.md.
