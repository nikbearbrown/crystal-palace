# NOTES — Sydenham water-tower massing study

Prop for the Crystal Palace film. A scale reference for
the shell builder — NOT a reconstruction of the towers.

## What it is

Two plain rectangular masses (20 m square, 70 m tall,
80 m centre-to-centre) with flat cap slabs. No taper, no
ornament, no water-tank detail: the prop deliberately
asserts no tower form at all, because no tower elevation
or dimension was found in the repo sources (checked
2026-10-26; local notes mention towers only as a
construction-state question and as an individual
demolition-date flag).

## What this prop must NOT be used for

- Scale-critical shots, until tower elevations are
  sourced and the masses are rescaled.
- Any demolition narrative: tower demolition dates and
  causes need individual records (storyboard flag); this
  prop asserts none.
- CP042 except in a date-supported state; CP062 (towers
  survive the fire) and CP064 (skyline loses its last
  markers — by explicit time cuts only) are its shots.
