# NOTES — Packing crate + exhibition vitrine

Prop for the Crystal Palace film. Hyde Park 1851 building only.

## What it is

Two objects side by side, one prop folder: a slatted packing
crate (left, x = −1.4 m) and a glazed exhibition vitrine on a
plinth base (right, x = +1.4 m). Origin at ground centre between
them. Units: metres. Blender 4.2+.

## Dimensions — all guessed

No crate or case dimension was found in the Downes controls or
the manifest image set checked for this prop. The Dickinson
plates (cases of goods, CC0, in `references/muse-drive/images/
commons/`) give the vitrine's *form* — glass box on a framed
base — not its measurements.

## What was NOT modelled

- No stencilled labels, maker's marks or shipping marks — any
  text would be invented.
- No exhibits inside the vitrine, no packing straw or
  protection inside the crate.
- No castors, handles or lifting gear.
