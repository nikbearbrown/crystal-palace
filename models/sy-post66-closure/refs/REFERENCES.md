## Text / dimension sources (in the repo, not copied here)

- `references/downes-1852/crystal-palace-1851/CONTROL-DIMENSIONS.md`
  and `models/hyde-park-column-tier1/NOTES.md` — the palace's
  structural dimensions used for the bay (24 ft module; 10 in
  column; girder 3 ft deep, soffit 19 ft 2 in). The Sydenham
  palace reused the same modular iron system.
- `models/hp-floor-boards/NOTES.md` — the palace floor's printed
  member sizes (Official Catalogue 1851, pp.62–63): boards
  1½ in thick, ½ in open gaps, joists 7 × 2½ in.
- `references/muse-round2/review/GAPS-FOUND.md` (branch
  `muse-round2`), S66/S67 sections and the plans table — the
  1856 guide's record (p.148) that the north transept was
  already partitioned from the nave by "a gigantic curtain" at
  the Tropical End a decade before the fire: the documented
  precedent for closing this end with a soft/partition screen.
