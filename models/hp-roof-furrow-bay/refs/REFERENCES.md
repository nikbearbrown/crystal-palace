# REFERENCES — hp-roof-furrow-bay

All references are already in the repo `nikbearbrown/crystal-palace`
(Hyde Park 1851 evidence only; no Sydenham material used). Copies of the
four plates and the two interior photographs are in this folder.

## Primary — construction drawings

- `references/downes-1852/source-scans/plates/plate-012.jpg` — Downes
  Plate XII, "Glass roof and Paxton gutters" (John Weale, June 1851).
  **The governing sheet.** Fig. 1 section of roof (8 ft pitch, 4 ft
  half-pitch printed); Fig. 3 elevation of Paxton gutter with the
  sash-bar notch chain dimensioned (5¼ in margins, 10½ in typical
  centres, 11¼ in flanking the wide notches, 24 ft overall); Fig. 10
  enlarged section through gutter and slopes: glass "Inclination
  2½ to 1", gutter channel 1¼ + 1¼ in deep, 5 in base, 6 in dimension;
  "Section of ordinary bar" (1 in wide, 1⅜ in deep, ⅜ in rib/groove
  figures); "Section of strong bar" (2½ in wide; 1⅞ / 2⅜ / 15⁄16 in
  figures, partly illegible); ridge piece G, 3 in wide at base.
  Licence: Public domain (published 1850s), per round-1 manifest.
  Crops saved here: `plate-012-fig10-gutter-and-bars.png`,
  `plate-012-fig3-bar-chain-left.png`, `plate-012-ridge-piece.png`.
- `references/downes-1852/source-scans/plates/plate-013.jpg` — Plate
  XIII, "Main gutters". The larger main gutters over the 24/48/72 ft
  spans (not this panel's small Paxton gutter); Fig. 5 gives the gutter
  fall, ⅝ in in 8 ft, quoted in NOTES as a not-modelled feature.
  Licence: Public domain (published 1850s).
- `references/downes-1852/source-scans/plates/plate-014.jpg` — Plate
  XIV, "Details of gallery, and ornamental bands on columns".
  Consulted; gallery construction only, nothing used for the roof
  panel. Licence: Public domain (published 1850s).
- `references/downes-1852/source-scans/plates/plate-018.jpg` — Plate
  XVIII, "Details of transept roof". Consulted: Figs. 7–9 show the
  transept vault's sash bars over the curved ribs — a different,
  curved construction; confirms sash bars as the standard glazing
  support but not used for this flat panel. Licence: Public domain
  (published 1850s).

## Dimension controls and text

- `references/downes-1852/crystal-palace-1851/CONTROL-DIMENSIONS.md` —
  D06 (24 ft primary unit, plate-checked/printed), D09 (8 ft gutter
  pitch, plate-checked on Plates VI and XII).
- `references/downes-1852/crystal-palace-1851/text/downes-1852-ocr-fulltext.txt`
  — Cowper's text: p.15 glass 49 in long, slope 2½ to 1 (printed-page
  check in CONTROL-DIMENSIONS; the OCR layer misreads it as "24 to 1");
  the "Glass Roof" / "Manufacture of the Sash Bars" passages — sash
  bars 1 in wide, 1⅜ in deep, grooved each side to receive the glass;
  three stronger bars 2½ in wide in every 24 ft, positions shown by the
  wide notches in Fig. 3; roof glass cut into panes 49 in long and
  10 in wide, weighing 16 oz per square foot.
