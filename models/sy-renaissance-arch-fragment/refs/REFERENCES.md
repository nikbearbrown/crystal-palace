# REFERENCES — sy-renaissance-arch-fragment

All sources are from the repo's own round-1 collection
(`references/muse-drive/`), copied here from the identical local
mirror of that collection. Licences are the round-1 manifest's
(`references/muse-drive/data/manifest.csv`). No source outside the
repo collection was used for geometry. Round 2
(`references/muse-round2/`, branch `muse-round2`) was also checked:
per the Gothic fragment's documented check, its only court
photograph is the Roman Court (S57-02) — a different court, not
used here.

## Primary source (form of the bay)

- `stereograph-italian-court.jpg` — stereograph of the Italian
  Court, Sydenham (stereo pair, 1221 × 645): the court's loggia
  arcade — round arches on piers with Ionic pilasters, a carved
  frieze, and the gallery above; centre-foreground the great
  tazza of the Gaillon fountain; against the arcade, a Medici
  tomb (seated Lorenzo with reclining figures). This photograph
  is the prop's form source and its scale anchor (see NOTES.md).
  Public domain (manifest). Repo path:
  `references/muse-drive/images/commons/CrystalPalaceItalianCourt.jpg`.
  Enlarged crops: `crop-stereo-left-eye.png` (the left eye, 3×),
  `crop-arcade.png` (4×), `crop-pier-capital.png` (6× — the Ionic
  volutes and bolster, the impost blocks, the archivolt roll and
  the spandrel roundels are legible here).

## Supporting sources (text and plan, same guide)

1856 *Guide to the Crystal Palace and Park* (Samuel Phillips),
"THE RENAISSANCE COURT", pp.95–99. Public domain (manifest).

- `guide-1856-p97-bourgtheroulde-loggia.jpg` — printed p.97: the
  entrance façade is "a restored copy of a portion of the Hôtel
  Bourgtheroulde, at Rouen"; "in the lunettes under the ceiling
  of the small loggia, or gallery, portraits of twelve of the
  most celebrated patrons of art"; "In the centre of the Court
  we find a fountain of the Renaissance period, from the Château
  de Gaillon" — the fountain in the stereograph, which is the
  identification chain tying the photograph to this court.
  Repo path: `.../Guide_to_the_Crystal_Palace_and_Park_1856_(147900487).jpg`.
- `guide-1856-p98-renaissance-court-plan.jpg` — printed p.98: the
  ground plan of the court, showing the arcade's place in the
  court's layout. Repo path:
  `.../Guide_to_the_Crystal_Palace_and_Park_1856_(147900489).jpg`.
- `guide-1856-p99-back-wall.jpg` — printed p.99: the back wall as
  an assemblage of copied exhibits (Certosa di Pavia compositions,
  Goujon doors, Doria Palace doorway, Ilaria di Caretto monument)
  — the evidence for the interpretation caveat in NOTES.md.
  Repo path: `.../Guide_to_the_Crystal_Palace_and_Park_1856_(147900490).jpg`.

## Checked and NOT used

- `guide-1856-p95-renaissance-entrance.jpg` — printed p.95, the
  chapter's woodcut "Entrance to Renaissance Court": segmental
  arches and dense carving — this is the court's **nave entrance
  screen** (the Bourgtheroulde copy of p.97's text), a different
  element from the loggia arcade the stereograph shows. Read and
  deliberately NOT used for the bay's form. Repo path:
  `.../Guide_to_the_Crystal_Palace_and_Park_1856_(147900484).jpg`.
- `medieval-vestibule-photo-rp-f-2012-96-334.jpg` — Rijksmuseum
  stereoscopic photograph, "The French and Italian Medieval
  Vestibule" (guide p.94) — a separate, Gothic, space. Consulted
  because the Gothic fragment's build used it; for this bay it
  contributes nothing but the methodological precedent (derive
  scale from a photographed known object, keep distinct spaces'
  evidence distinct). NOT used for form or dimension. CC0
  (manifest). Repo path:
  `references/muse-drive/images/commons/Beeldhouwwerken_in_de_Franse_en_Italiaanse_Middeleeuwse_voorhal_van_Crystal_Palace_in_Sydenham_Crystal_Palace._4._The_French_and_Italian_Medieval_Vestibule._(titel_op_object),_RP-F-2012-96-334.jpg`.
- Rijksmuseum stereophotograph RP-F-1999-106, whose printed
  verso reads "CRYSTAL PALACE. / 47. VIEW IN THE RENAISSANCE
  COURT." (CC0; dated ca. 1862–ca. 1875 in the manifest) — a
  captioned view in this very court exists, but the round-1
  manifest records it as **skipped-budget: it was catalogued,
  not downloaded**, so it is not in the collection and could not
  be used. Noted so the gap is explicit; the form rests on the
  stereograph above.
- Hyde Park material: none was used, under the Sydenham-only
  rule — the same rule on which the Gothic fragment's build
  excluded Pugin's 1851 Mediaeval Court image (MET DP258011).
  The Italian Court is a Sydenham creation of 1854; the 1851
  building had no such court.
- Delamotte photographs: no view of the Italian/Renaissance
  Court is in the round-1 collection under his name (searched
  by filename and manifest); the stereograph above is the
  court's photographic record in the collection.
