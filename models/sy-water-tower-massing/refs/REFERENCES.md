two measurement crops are in this folder; everything else is cited
to its path in the repo (or to the round-2 package on branch
`muse-round2`).

## Text / record sources

- **Round-2 review, `references/muse-round2/review/GAPS-FOUND.md`
  (branch `muse-round2`), §S51** — collects the tower facts:
  86 m / 282–284 ft; 12 cast-iron columns on a concrete ring
  (Historic England); towers begun Aug 1854, operational
  2 June 1856 (Historic England); no openly licensed Brunel drawing
  of the towers exists (ICE holds material, no open licence).
- **GAPS-FOUND, contradictions §9** — demolition record: south
  tower dismantled in sections, autumn 1940; north tower blown up
  16 April 1941 (SAS account + Historic England listing); tower
  height "variously 280 ft (ILN 1941), 282 ft, 284 ft, 86 m —
  unresolved."
- **`models/SCENE-BUILD-PLAN.md` item 18** — the build brief:
  massing study only, shell-builder aid; scenes CP042 / CP062 /
  CP064.
- **Routledge (1854) pp.25–26 and Phillips (1854) p.28** (round-2
  images `PLAN-1854-routledge-dimensions-p25/p26.jpg`,
  `PLAN-1854-phillips-dimensions-p28.jpg`) — checked for a printed
  tower height: their dimension chapters cover the palace only and
  print **no tower figure** (the towers were only begun in
  August 1854). The 1856 guide's dimension table
  (`references/muse-drive/review/dimensions-from-1856-guide.json`)
  likewise carries no tower entry. So no period printed tower
  height exists in the collection; see the conflict in NOTES.md.
