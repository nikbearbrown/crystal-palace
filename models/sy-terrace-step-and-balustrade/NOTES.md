# NOTES — Sydenham terrace step + balustrade section

Prop for the Crystal Palace film. Sydenham 1854–1936 building
only — never Hyde Park, never a specific dated terrace state.

## What it is

One step flight with a balustrade on both sides and a
truncated top landing, reusable up and down a terrace slope.
The flight ascends along +Y. Origin at the centre of the
bottom step's bottom face. Units: metres. Blender 4.2+.

## Dimensions — all guessed

No surveyed terrace dimension was used. The 1857 map (image
lead 0591) gives terrace position, not step sizes, and no
modern terrace survey was consulted for measurements.

| Part | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Steps | 6, 0.30 m going, 0.15 m rise | Guessed |
| Flight width | 3.0 m | Guessed |
| Step construction | Solid blocks, each step spanning its full 0.15 m rise | Modelling choice — QC caught 0.12 m slabs leaving air gaps under each tread; solid masonry is the honest read |
| Handrail height | 0.90 m above nosing line | Guessed |
| Baluster pitch | 0.30 m along the slope | Guessed |
| Baluster section | 0.07 m square | Guessed |
| Landing depth | 1.0 m | Modelling choice — truncated, real terraces run longer |
| Stone colour | Pale grey, darker rails/newels | Interpretive, so the balustrade reads against the steps |

## What was NOT modelled

- No specific terrace level, no survey profile, no surviving
  fabric claim — this section does not assert which terrace
  it is, or its condition in any given year.
- No fountains, basins, sphinxes or planting — fountains are
  a separate, date-gated prop (Wave 3, item 15); this prop does
  not imply the waterworks were running.
- No baluster turning profile — square balusters are a
  simplification, not a claim about the real balustrade form.

## Relationship to other models

Independent prop. It pairs with the present-day terrace walk
(CP073) in modern condition only if re-dressed — this version
is the clean, undamaged section. It must not be placed in
1854 (CP035 excludes later terraces) or before the terrace
state it is dressed for.
