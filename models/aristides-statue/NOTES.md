# Aristides (or Aeschines) statue — build notes

The standing draped orator of the **Greek & Roman sculpture court,
Sydenham** — the cast the court's own plinth lettering calls "ARISTIDES
OR AESCHINES / FROM NAPLES" (the Naples Aeschines type). Candidate #6 in
`../CANDIDATES.md`. Sydenham state only (the court as photographed;
the stereograph is dated c.1870–c.1890 in the manifest). Film scene
CP036. The statue is modelled **on its own white base slab and on the
court plinth**, as photographed; the plinth alone is also available as
`../display-plinth/` (candidate #35) and `../sy-classical-cast-base/`.

The figure is the scale anchor two earlier builds hang off this same
photograph (the krater's 1.30 m and the plinth's 0.90 m both derive
from taking this cast as ~2.0 m). This build keeps that anchor so all
three props agree in a scene.

## Orientation — read before placing

The photograph shows the statue from its **left side**, in near-perfect
profile, standing side-on to the aisle: the plinth's lettered face and
the statue's profile face the same way. The model keeps that
relationship:

- the statue's anatomical front (chest, toes) faces **−X**;
- its photographed left side, and the plinth's lettered face, face **−Y**;
- renders: `front.png` = the statue's front (−X, **inferred** — never
  photographed); `side.png` = the documented profile (−Y);
  `three-quarter.png` from (−X, −Y), close to the photograph's own
  viewpoint; `profile.png` is a tighter framing of the profile and
  `compare-side.jpg` sets it beside the photograph.

## Dimensions and where they came from

Overall assembly: **3.05 m high**; footprint 1.00 × 0.65 m (the
statue slab's top plate; plinth foot 0.90 × 0.65 m). Origin at base
centre; built in metres, scale applied. **20,332 triangles**, 51
objects.

**Ratios are measured from the photograph; the absolute scale is the
shared inferred anchor.** Landmarks were picked on the left half of
the stereograph at native resolution (617 px = 1 m at the adopted
scale):

- Figure (soles to crown) = 1,234 px → **2.00 m adopted**. This is the
  display-plinth build's anchor (plinth ÷ statue = 0.454 in frame →
  0.90 m plinth), cross-checked by the krater-vase build (same figure,
  1,345 px on its picks) and by `sy-classical-cast-base` (ratio 0.468 →
  statue 1.94 m against its 0.91 m pedestal). All three agree within
  ~3%; read absolute dimensions as **±10%**.
- Crown to chin = 183 px ≈ 0.30 m (0.148 of figure height).
- Chest knot centre at 0.70 of figure height; belly front stands
  0.118 m in front of the hem's front line; torso depth at the neck
  ≈ 0.29 m; hanging flap tip 0.28 m above the soles, its back face
  ≈ 0.15 m behind the body's back line at that height; foot 0.276 m
  long, toe 0.216 m in front of the hem line.
- The statue's own slab: front face measures 111 px ≈ 0.18 m in frame
  (includes perspective magnification — the feet stand back from its
  front edge); **0.15 m adopted**, 0.98 × 0.62 m with a 1.00 × 0.64 m
  top plate.
- Plinth **0.90 m high, foot 0.90 × 0.65 m, top slab 0.84 × 0.59 m,
  base-moulding zone 0.185 m** — taken from `../display-plinth/`,
  which measured this very plinth off this same stereograph.

## Measured / seen in the photograph

- A standing male figure **entirely wrapped in a heavy himation**:
  columnar draped mass, a bunched knot of cloth clutched at the
  sternum, a thick rolled edge (the himation's roll) sweeping
  diagonally from the chest down the left side to the back, a shallow
  hanging scoop of cloth on the left side bottoming at mid-thigh
  (the cloth over the wrapped left forearm), and the himation's end
  hanging as a flat flap behind the legs to 0.28 m above the soles,
  its lower corner pointed.
- Head in profile: **balding crown, hair mass with curls at the side
  and nape, a beard**, straight nose, short neck; the head inclines
  slightly forward. Shoulders slope under the drapery; slight forward
  lean of the whole mass; the belly is the frontmost point of the
  torso silhouette.
- Both feet in sandals project from the hem, the left advanced;
  drapery hem clears the ankles.
- Between/behind the feet stands a **small block-like object** whose
  front face shows a band of small arch shapes above vertical
  striations (see "weakest" below).
- The statue stands on its **own white slab**; the slab's front face
  is numbered **"324"**; the black plinth's painted lettering reads
  **"325 / ARISTIDES OR AESCHINES / FROM NAPLES"**. (Earlier notes in
  this repo quote "320" for this plinth; this stereo half reads 325,
  agreeing with `sy-classical-cast-base`. The model carries the
  recessed unlettered label plate of the display-plinth build — no
  lettering is modelled, here as there.)

## Guessed / inferred — not seen

- **The whole front and right side.** Widths (shoulders 0.46 m, hem
  0.39 m) come from the classical type and the profile depths, not
  from any photograph. The diagonal roll's path across the chest,
  the knot's exact form, and the right arm's disposition (wholly
  inside the drapery, as the type dictates) are inference.
- **The back**, except the hanging flap and the shoulder line the
  profile shows.
- **Face and hands are schematic** at this stylisation level: nose,
  beard mass and hair are profile-driven; there are no eyes, fingers
  or sandal strap detail beyond one carved strap band.
- **The support block is the least-resolved element.** At native
  resolution it is a striated block with an arcaded top band, ~0.22 m
  high, standing between the feet. Whether it is a marble support, a
  scroll-box (capsa) or another fitting cannot be settled from this
  photograph; it is modelled as a plain block with five arch bumps
  and six flutes on its front face and should be read as "object
  present", not as an identification.
- The flap's twist (its broad face turned ~24° from the body's back
  plane) is inferred from its projected width in the photograph.
- Plinth profile details (cap step, chamfer) follow the
  display-plinth build's reading of the same photograph.
- Materials are flat plaster/ebonised tones for the grey rig; the
  stereograph is a sepia albumen print, so exact tones are an
  interpretation.

## Build

Procedural bpy (Blender 4.2), script in `scripts/`; the drapery is a
64-segment lofted profile with harmonic fold displacement, plus tube
geometry for the himation roll and side scoop, a solidified panel for
the hanging flap, and lofted feet. Five build rounds, four of them
iterating on the profile against the stereograph (assembly scale bug
found and fixed in round 1; swag rebuilt onto the surface in round 4
after a mis-measured hanging depth). Renders are Cycles CPU on the
fixed grey rig in `scripts/render_rig.py` (Eevee cannot initialise in
this VM — no EGL); the same rig frames every view.

**Weakest points, honestly:** the head (profile features only,
schematic face), the front view (wholly inferred), and the support
block (present but unidentified). The profile — the one view the film
can check against the photograph — is the strong one.
