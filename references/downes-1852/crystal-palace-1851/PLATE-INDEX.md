Notes

- The JP2 page number is the book-scan page, not the plate number. Raw pages are in
  `source-scans/pages/.../` (git-ignored).
- Plates 1–7 are the whole-building plans, elevations and sections, so they are the main
  measuring sheets for the dimension gate. Plates 8–27 are the details.
- The plates were scanned with glass weights holding the paper; the weights sit outside the
  drawing area but the paper edge is curved, so lengths measured near the edges can be off by
  a few percent. Prefer measuring against the same sheet's own scale bar.
- Plate 20a's exact title was not read; "Details of cast-iron standards" is from the
  figure captions ("Details of Cast-iron Standard A… B and C… D").
