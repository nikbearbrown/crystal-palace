## Why the EPUB is not enough

The EPUB was generated from the scan's text layer. Its image set keeps only some crops of
each page, many of them fold-backs or margins. Examples: `image_0074_00` and `_01` and `_02`
are three different crops around one folded plate (a blank fold-back, the semicircular
transept head, and a bay elevation); `image_0066` is a 9,000 px strip of a plan.
