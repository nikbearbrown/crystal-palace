## Source distinctions

The Downes collection concerns the 1851 Hyde Park building. The supplied FBX conversion is a separate asset; its identity and historical dimensions have not been verified against Downes. Keep their models and evidence separate until that comparison is made.

The original architecture project labels its model as Claude's work. Muse's separate collection was subsequently identified by Nik Bear Brown through the supplied Google Drive folder and reviewed on October 24, 2026. It is preserved separately at [muse-drive/](muse-drive/), with source attribution intact.

## Included and omitted

Included: all 28 working plates, the full original JP2 ZIP, scan metadata, original EPUB, OCR, measurement documentation and scripts, latest recorded working model and model scripts, the complete linked course package, local reference-image leads, and existing history notes. The EPUB has incomplete plate imagery and is retained as a secondary copy. Raw JP2 files already contained in the ZIP, obsolete model snapshots, caches, and film/audio renders were not duplicated. Scan-rights caveats in the source notes are retained. Human fact/visual review is still pending where the source notes say so.
